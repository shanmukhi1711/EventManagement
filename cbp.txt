# Design Thinking Project: VNR VJIET Spark Event Management Web Application

## Problem Statement
VNR VJIET hosts diverse campus events, but fragmented manual communication via posters, emails, and word-of-mouth hinders student awareness and participation. Students struggle to discover events, register efficiently, and track their involvement, leading to missed opportunities for engagement and skill development. Event organizers face administrative burdens in managing registrations and generating certificates without a centralized system. This results in low participation rates and inefficient processes that impede campus culture and student growth. A digital platform is needed to streamline event discovery, registration, notifications, and certificate management, enhancing accessibility and fostering a vibrant community at VNR VJIET.

## Empathy Tool Used
Interviews and surveys were used to empathize with users. Semi-structured interviews gathered deep qualitative insights into pain points, while surveys provided quantitative data on preferences and behaviors.

## Research Conducted
Primary research included semi-structured interviews with 20 students and 5 faculty members to gather qualitative insights on event experiences. A survey questionnaire was distributed to 100+ students via Google Forms, collecting quantitative data on participation rates and preferences (e.g., 70% prefer digital notifications). Stakeholder groups involved: Students (primary users), Faculty/Organizers (secondary), and Administration (tertiary). Secondary research reviewed statistics from similar campus apps, showing average participation increases of 40% with digital tools.

- What features would you prioritize in a digital event platform (e.g., notifications, easy registration, certificates)?
- How important is mobile access for event discovery?
- Would you prefer personalized recommendations based on your interests?
- What security concerns do you have with online registration?
- How can the platform help build community among students?
- What integrations (e.g., Google Calendar, social media) would be useful?
- How should the platform handle event analytics for organizers?

## Ideation Method Used
Brainstorming was chosen as the best suitable ideation method for this web application. It allows rapid generation of diverse ideas in a collaborative group setting, focusing on user-centric features like event discovery, registration, and notifications. This method encourages creativity while ensuring ideas align with technical and economic constraints of a campus web app.

## Solution Prototype
The prototype is the developed web application "VNR VJIET Spark," built using React (frontend) and Node.js/Express (backend) with SQLite database. It includes user authentication (Google OAuth), event listings, registration forms, profile pages, and certificate generation. The prototype was designed using Figma for initial wireframes and then implemented as a functional app. Key screens: Home page with event carousel, Events page with filters, Login/Register, Profile with registered events. The app is responsive and accessible via desktop/mobile. Testing involved user feedback loops to iterate on usability.

## Innovation in Terms of Desirability, Viability, and Feasibility
- **Desirability:** High user appeal through intuitive UI/UX, personalized features, and mobile-first design, addressing real pain points like poor event visibility.
- **Viability:** Financially sustainable as a free campus tool; potential for monetization via premium features for organizers. Market validation from surveys shows 85% willingness to adopt.
- **Feasibility:** Technically achievable with open-source stacks (React, Node.js); development time was 4-6 weeks with a small team. Scalable for future enhancements like AI recommendations.

## Design for Environment (Optional)
The solution promotes sustainability by digitizing event management, reducing paper waste from posters, flyers, and printed certificates. Digital notifications and online registrations minimize physical resources, aligning with eco-friendly campus initiatives. The app's cloud-based hosting ensures low energy consumption compared to traditional methods, contributing to VNR VJIET's green goals.
